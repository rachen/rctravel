Weather & What It Means

August is the hottest, wettest month across this entire route. Two things genuinely affect plans: heat in Japan, and typhoon risk in Vietnam.
Leg Temps Conditions Planning impact
TokyoAug 6–11 26–33°Cfeels 38–42°C Hot, humid, ~7 rainy days Outdoor stuff before 9 AM or after 6:30 PM
HakoneAug 11–12 Cooler at elevation Mountain air, some rain Best-timed stop on the trip — indoors and onsen anyway
Kyoto / OsakaAug 12–15 24–35°Chottest of the Japan leg Kyoto's basin geography traps heat Fushimi Inari early is essential, not optional. Obon Aug 13–16 = crowds
HanoiAug 15–18 26–33°C~82% humidity Wettest month — 275–310mm, 15–22 rainy days, peak typhoon season Cruise cancellation risk is real. Keep indoor backups ready
Ha Long / Lan Ha Bay cruise — genuine risk: late July to mid-September is the peak of northern Vietnam's typhoon season, and bay sailings get pulled on 12–24 hours' notice when a storm enters the Gulf of Tonkin. Vietnam's forecasting center flagged 3–4 significant systems possible for August alone. Two things worth doing before departure: confirm Hera's cancellation and refund terms, and keep Ninh Binh in your back pocket — it's inland, largely unaffected by bay closures, and was the original plan for that day.
Obon overlap: Japan's Obon holiday falls Aug 13–16 this year, right across your Kyoto stay and Osaka day trip. Expect heavier domestic crowds at Fushimi Inari, Nara, and Dotonbori, and book restaurants further ahead than usual. Trains between Kyoto and Osaka will also be busier than normal.

Transport Notes

Suica cards: pick up at an HND kiosk or vending machine on arrival — covers trains, buses, and konbini purchases everywhere in Japan, no need to buy individual tickets
HND → hotel: taxi or Limousine Bus, skip trains after the long flight in
Tokyo → Hakone: Booked — Romancecar "Hakone 21," 1:20 PM Shinjuku → 2:48 PM Hakone-Yumoto, seats 6C/6D, control #E447
Luggage forwarding: ship main bags from Tokyo hotel to Kyoto hotel via takkyubin (Yamato Transport) the day before Hakone — travel light with just an overnight bag
Hakone → Kyoto: train to Odawara, then Shinkansen to Kyoto — about an hour total
Kyoto ↔ Osaka: Shinkansen, ~15 min each way — day trip, no hotel change needed
